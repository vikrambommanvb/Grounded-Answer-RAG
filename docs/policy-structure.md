**Consolidated Text as at 31 December 2025**

## 1. Corpus Overview & Hierarchy

The Calder County Household Support Program (HSP) Policy Manual comprises **twelve Parts**, containing **54 sections** and **148 uniquely numbered clauses**. 

The hierarchy follows a strict three-tier numerical notation:
$$\text{Part } X \longrightarrow \text{Section } X.Y \longrightarrow \text{Paragraph/Clause } \S X.Y.Z$$

Where:
- $X \in \{1, \dots, 12\}$ represents the major thematic domain.
- $Y$ represents a functional sub-domain.
- $Z$ represents an individual enforceable rule, definition, condition, or limitation.
- Letter suffixes (e.g., $10.5.3\text{A}$) denote inserted amendment provisions.

## 2. Exhaustive Mapping of Parts and Clauses

### Part 1: Scope and Definitions (§1.1.1 – §1.4.11)
*Governs statutory authority, manual structure, interpretation canons, and baseline terminology.*

- **§1.1 Purpose of the Program**
  - **§1.1.1** Mandate: Monthly financial assistance for essential living costs + associated support services.
  - **§1.1.2** Administration: Department of Household Services ("the Department") via four district offices.
  - **§1.1.3** Discretionary Canon: No entitlement beyond statute; discretion must be exercised reasonably and recorded in case file.
- **§1.2 Structure of this manual**
  - **§1.2.1** Twelve-part division summary (Eligibility: Parts 1–5; Award: 6–7; Ongoing Admin: 8–10; Review/Appeal: 11–12).
  - **§1.2.2** Cross-reference citation syntax rule (§X.Y.Z).
  - **§1.2.3** Amendment rule: Numbered amendments take effect on stated effective date; applies to determinations made on/after effective date unless provided otherwise.
- **§1.3 Interpretation**
  - **§1.3.1** Modal Verbs: "must" (mandatory), "may" (discretion), "should" (ordinary practice departure requires reason).
  - **§1.3.2** Time Computation: Days = calendar days. If last day is an office closure, period extends to next open day.
  - **§1.3.3** Monetary Rule: Monetary amounts are those in force at the date of determination.
- **§1.4 Definitions**
  - **§1.4.1 Applicant:** Person who applied for assistance not yet determined.
  - **§1.4.2 Recipient:** Person in receipt of assistance.
  - **§1.4.3 Household:** Applicant/recipient + persons ordinarily residing at same address sharing living arrangements (excludes separate tenancies/licences).
  - **§1.4.4 Household member:** Any person in §1.4.3 other than applicant/recipient.
  - **§1.4.5 Dependent child:** Person under 18 in household, or 18–20 enrolled in secondary education.
  - **§1.4.6 Full-time student:** Person enrolled in higher education classified as full-time by the institution (contact hours immaterial).
  - **§1.4.7 Countable income:** Part 6 income not disregarded under §6.4.
  - **§1.4.8 Change of circumstances:** Any change in facts underlying eligibility or award.
  - **§1.4.9 Determination:** Departmental decision on entitlement, amount, suspension, or termination.
  - **§1.4.10 District office:** Four designated locations: Calder Central, Northgate, Weybridge, Ash Hill.
  - **§1.4.11 Working day:** Day on which district office is open to public.

### Part 2: General Conditions of Eligibility (§2.1.1 – §2.4.3)
*Sets cumulative conditions for household entitlement.*

- **§2.1 The basic conditions**
  - **§2.1.1** Temporal Point: Must satisfy conditions at date of determination.
  - **§2.1.2** Cumulative Conditions (a–f):
    - (a) Resident in Calder County (Part 3);
    - (b) Aged 18+, or satisfies §2.3;
    - (c) Countable income $\le$ Part 6 threshold;
    - (d) Countable resources $\le$ §2.4 limit;
    - (e) Not excluded under Part 4;
    - (f) Valid application submitted under Part 8.
  - **§2.1.3** Household Assessment Principle: One application and one award per household.
- **§2.2 Continuing eligibility**
  - **§2.2.1** Continuity: Must be satisfied throughout award period. Loss of condition ends eligibility from that date.
  - **§2.2.2** Periodic Review: Re-evaluated at intervals not exceeding 12 months.
- **§2.3 Applicants under 18**
  - **§2.3.1** Exception Criteria: Age 16–17 eligible if: (a) not member of another household, and (b) no parent willing/able to support, OR (c) parent of residing dependent child.
  - **§2.3.2** Referral Gate: Must refer to supervisor before determination.
- **§2.4 Resources**
  - **§2.4.1** Absolute Ceiling: Total countable household resources must not exceed **$4,000**.
  - **§2.4.2** Exempt Resources: (a) Principal home; (b) One vehicle per household; (c) Household goods/effects; (d) Child education savings account; (e) Life insurance surrender value $\le \$1,500$.
  - **§2.4.3** Joint Resources: Apportioned by beneficial interest (equal division presumed if unknown).

---

### Part 3: Residence (§3.1.1 – §3.3.2)
*Governs territorial nexus and temporary absence.*

- **§3.1 The residence condition**
  - **§3.1.1** Test: Ordinary residence in Calder County + intent to remain.
  - **§3.1.2** Nature: Question of fact; physical presence on application day neither necessary nor sufficient.
  - **§3.1.3** No Fixed Address: Satisfies condition if connection established under §3.3.
- **§3.2 Temporary absence from the County**
  - **§3.2.1** Base Absence: Eligibility continues for first 28 days of absence.
  - **§3.2.2** Extended Absence (up to 90 days): Permitted for (a) medical treatment unavailable in county, (b) accompanying medical treatment recipient, or (c) deceased relative affairs.
  - **§3.2.3** Student Exclusion: Absence for full-time education explicitly excluded ("addressed separately").
  - **§3.2.4** Absence Overstay: Exceeding allowable period terminates eligibility from day after expiry, unless intent to return + outside control.
- **§3.3 Applicants with no fixed address**
  - **§3.3.1** Proof of Connection: (a) 30+ days in County immediately prior; (b) GP/shelter registration; or (c) recognized support agency confirmation.
  - **§3.3.2** Mail Collection: May collect mail at district office; non-collection is NOT a failure to respond under §8.6.

---

### Part 4: Exclusions (§4.1.1 – §4.3.4)
*Disqualifications, institutional care, and reporting obligations.*

- **§4.1 Excluded persons**
  - **§4.1.1** Disqualification Grounds: (a) Unexpired sanction (§10.5); (b) Correctional detention; (c) Receipt of equivalent external program aid; (d) Deliberate misrepresentation bar (§9.6).
  - **§4.1.2** Partial Exclusion Rule: Excluded member's needs are removed from Part 7, but their income remains countable under Part 6.
- **§4.2 Persons in residential care**
  - **§4.2.1** Permanent Care: Ceases to be household member once residence is permanent.
  - **§4.2.2** Temporary Care: Treated as temporary for first 56 days.
- **§4.3 Recipient obligations**
  - **§4.3.1** Duties: Provide info, attend interview (§8.5), notify changes (§4.3.2), repay overpayments (Part 9).
  - **§4.3.2** Change Reporting Timeline (Original Text): Report within **10 calendar days** of change or awareness. *(Amended to 14 days by Amd 2026-01)*.
  - **§4.3.3** Reporting Channels: In person, phone, writing, online. Date received governs.
  - **§4.3.4** Consequences of Default: Sanction under §10.5 and overpayment recovery under Part 9.

---

### Part 5: Special Household Circumstances (§5.1.1 – §5.5.2)
*Specific living arrangements and household member profiles.*

- **§5.1 Residential care recalculation:** Temporary (unchanged); permanent (recalculate under §4.2).
- **§5.2 Temporarily absent member:** Remains member for 28 days (or 90 days under §3.2.2 reasons). §5.2.3 excludes full-time education.
- **§5.3 Training allowance:** 100% disregarded under §6.4; recipient remains household member.
- **§5.4 Care allowance:** Disregarded up to $200/mo under §6.4.1(f); does not alter household composition. *(Note: Referenced incorrectly by §7.1.3 as covering full-time students).*
- **§5.5 Immigration conditions:** Recourse-prohibited member's needs excluded, income disregarded. **Must refer to supervisor (§5.5.2)**.

---

### Part 6: Income (§6.1.1 – §6.6.1)
*Rules for calculating countable income and eligibility thresholds.*

- **§6.1 General:** Total income of all members minus §6.4 disregards. Counted when received.
- **§6.2 Countable income:** Employment earnings, self-employment profit, pensions, adult maintenance, net rental, other government aid.
- **§6.3 Irregular/Lump-sum:** Lump sum = resource (§2.4), unless income arrears (§6.3.2). Irregular earnings averaged over 13 weeks.
- **§6.4 Disregards:**
  - §6.4.1(a) **Earnings disregard:** First **$120/month** of household earnings *(Amended to $175/mo by Amd 2026-01)*.
  - §6.4.1(b) Child support for dependent child (100% disregarded).
  - §6.4.1(c) Training allowance (100% disregarded).
  - §6.4.1(d) Disability payments (100% disregarded).
  - §6.4.1(e) Irregular charitable gifts (100% disregarded).
  - §6.4.1(f) Care allowance (up to $200/month disregarded).
  - §6.4.1(g) Dependent child income (100% disregarded).
  - §6.4.2 Disregard applies once per household, not per earner.
- **§6.5 Self-employment:** Net profit = gross receipts minus necessary business expenses. Capital, depreciation, and member payments disallowed.
- **§6.6 Income thresholds (Original table):**
  - Household 1: $1,180 | 2: $1,590 | 3: $2,000 | 4: $2,410 | 5: $2,820 | Additional: +$410.

---

### Part 7: Calculation of the Award (§7.1.1 – §7.4.3)
*Mathematical determination of monthly benefit.*

- **§7.1 The award formula:**
  $$\text{Award} = \text{Applicable Needs Figure (§7.2)} - \text{Countable Income}$$
  - Minimum Award Rule (§7.1.2): If resulting figure is $< \$25$, award = $0.
  - Draftsmanship Defect (§7.1.3): States full-time students are treated under §5.4 (false cross-reference).
- **§7.2 Needs figures (Original table):**
  - Single adult: $1,240
  - Couple: $1,670
  - Each dependent child: + $330
  - Single adult with dependent children: $1,480 base
  - Excluded members omitted (§7.2.2).
- **§7.3 Needs Adjustments:**
  - §7.3.1 Daily Living Assistance (ADL $\ge 2$): + $90/month.
  - §7.3.2 Child under age 2: + $140/month.
  - §7.3.3 External housing assistance: Reduced dollar-for-dollar.
- **§7.4 Payment:**
  - §7.4.1 Paid monthly in arrears.
  - §7.4.2 Bank transfer or prepaid card.
  - §7.4.3 **Apportionment:** Period of less than one month is apportioned by reference to the number of days.

---

### Part 8: Applications and Determinations (§8.1.1 – §8.7.2)
*Procedural lifecycle of claims.*

- **§8.1 Application:** Channels (online, in person, phone, writing). Valid if name, address, composition, signature. 30-day grace period to complete incomplete applications.
- **§8.2 Evidence:** Identity, residence, income, resources. Alternative evidence must be considered (§8.2.2). Minimum 14 days to provide evidence + extensions (§8.2.3).
- **§8.3 Time limits:** Must determine within 30 days. Written notification if delayed. **Supervisor referral if undetermined after 90 days (§8.3.3)**.
- **§8.4 Interim payments:** Permitted if delay $>30$ days not due to applicant. Recoverable only if false info.
- **§8.5 Interviews:** Discretionary on 10 days' notice. Companion permitted.
- **§8.6 Failure to respond:** Refusal or suspension requires at least 2 attempts across 2 different communication methods recorded on file.
- **§8.7 Notification:** Written notice must detail decision, reasons, calculation, review rights. Takes effect from date stated.

---

### Part 9: Overpayments and Recovery (§9.1.1 – §9.6.2)
*Establishment, clawback limits, waivers, and penalties.*

- **§9.1 Establishing overpayment:** Definition and causes.
  - **§9.1.4 Contradiction Trap:** Grants immunity for period before Department was in position to act if reported within *"30 calendar days required under §4.3"*. *(Contradicts §4.3.2 10-day rule in pre-amendment corpus).*
  - §9.1.5 Pure Departmental error non-recoverable if recipient could not reasonably know.
- **§9.2 Amount recoverable:** Difference between paid and proper entitlement; must apply offsetting adjustments (§9.2.2).
- **§9.3 Recovery:** Deduction max 10% of monthly award (20% for fraud). Hardship rate reduction.
- **§9.4 Waiver:** Permitted for serious hardship, uneconomic recovery, or inequitable departmental error.
- **§9.5 Time limits:** Overpayment must be established within 6 years of payment. Once established, recovery has no time limit.
- **§9.6 Deliberate misrepresentation:** 20% deduction + exclusion up to 13 weeks (1st offence) or 26 weeks (subsequent). **Must be determined by supervisor (§9.6.2)**.

---

### Part 10: Suspension, Termination and Sanctions (§10.1.1 – §10.5.4)
*Disciplinary mechanisms and protections.*

- **§10.1 Termination:** Condition loss ends award immediately. Death terminates award; survivors have 30 days to reapply with backdated effective date.
- **§10.2 Suspension:** Failure to respond, suspected ineligibility, or unknown whereabouts. **Supervisor referral required if unresolved after 60 days (§10.2.3)**. Arrears paid on reinstatement.
- **§10.3 Reinstatement:** Restored if terminated in error, or if condition re-established within 30 days.
- **§10.4 Household effect:** Individual sanction does not automatically extinguish household award.
- **§10.5 Sanctions:**
  - §10.5.1 Grounds: Failure to report changes within §4.3.2, interview non-attendance, condition breach.
  - §10.5.2 Rate (Original): **20% reduction** for 4 weeks (1st) or 8 weeks (repeat). *(Amended to 15% by Amd 2026-01)*.
  - §10.5.3 **Vulnerable Protections:** Sanction strictly prohibited if household includes child under 2, or member needing assistance with $\ge 2$ ADLs.
  - §10.5.4 Good cause defense: Illness, family emergency, system failure, reasonable barrier.

---

### Part 11: Review (§11.1.1 – §11.4.2)
*First-tier internal administrative dispute resolution.*

- **§11.1 Right:** Request within 30 days of §8.7 notice. Any form (including oral). Good cause extension.
- **§11.2 Conduct:** Must be independent officer uninvolved in original decision. Whole case reconsidered. 30-day completion limit.
- **§11.3 Outcome:** Confirm, vary, set aside. Written notice with Part 12 appeal rights.
- **§11.4 Pending review:** Discretionary continuation of award pending review.

---

### Part 12: Appeal (§12.1.1 – §12.3.3)
*Independent administrative tribunal.*

- **§12.1 Right:** Appeal to Calder County Assistance Appeals Panel within 30 days of review outcome. Review is prerequisite unless 30-day review limit lapsed.
- **§12.2 Procedure:** Panel sets procedure; right to appear, have representation, call witnesses. File provided 10 days before hearing.
- **§12.3 Decisions:** Binding on Department; written reasons within 14 days; Department must implement within 14 days.

## 3. Structural Defect Analysis & Traps

The policy manual contains intentional real-world defects that require deterministic software handling:

```
                                STRUCTURAL DEFECTS WEB
                                
   [§4.3.2 Original] ---------------- CONFLICT ----------------- [§9.1.4 Original]
  "within 10 calendar days"                                   "within 30 calendar days
                                                              required under §4.3"
          |                                                             |
          +-------------------- RESOLVED ON 1 MARCH 2026 ---------------+
                               (Both aligned to 14 days)
                               
                                
   [§7.1.3 Needs Calc] ----------- ERRONEOUS REF -------------> [§5.4 Care Allowance]
  "except in the case of                                      "Disregards care allowance
  full-time students (see §5.4)"                              up to $200... does not alter
                                                              household composition"
          |                                                             |
          +---------------------- APPARENT GAP -------------------------+
                         (No student needs formula exists!)
```

### 3.1 Defect 1: The Reporting Period Conflict (§4.3.2 vs. §9.1.4)
- **Pre-Amendment State (Determinations or changes prior to 1 March 2026):**
  - Section 4.3.2 establishes the affirmative obligation: recipient *must* report changes within 10 calendar days.
  - Section 9.1.4 establishes a safe harbor against overpayment recovery if reported within the *"30 calendar days required under §4.3"*.
  - This is a direct statutory draftsmanship contradiction.
  - **System Behavior:** A query asking what reporting deadline protects a recipient from overpayment for a pre-March change must not pick one arbitrarily. It must explicitly cite both §4.3.2 and §9.1.4, identify the internal conflict, and advise supervisor escalation.
- **Post-Amendment State (Changes occurring on/after 1 March 2026):**
  - Amendment No. 2026-01 aligns both clauses to **14 calendar days**.

### 3.2 Defect 2: The Full-Time Student Gap (§1.4.6, §3.2.3, §5.2.3, §7.1.3)
- Section 1.4.6 defines a full-time student.
- Section 3.2.3 and Section 5.2.3 both state that temporary absence does not include full-time education, stating it is "addressed separately".
- Section 7.1.3 explicitly directs caseworkers: *"The needs figure is calculated by reference to household size and composition, except in the case of full-time students (see §5.4)"*.
- Section 5.4 contains exclusively care allowance rules. Nowhere in Part 5 (or any other Part) is there a provision calculating a full-time student's needs or defining their entitlement.
- **System Behavior:** When queried regarding full-time student allowances or needs calculations, the system must issue an explicit **REFUSAL**. It must cite §7.1.3 and §5.4, explain that §5.4 fails to address students, and direct the caseworker to the District Office Supervisor or policy leadership.

## 4. Supervisory Escalation Matrix

The manual prescribes mandatory supervisor referral for five specific procedural junctures. The Grounded Answer must detect these situations and emit explicit warnings:

| Trigger Situation | Governing Clause | Mandatory Action |
|:---|:---|:---|
| Applicant aged 16 or 17 | **§2.3.2** | Application must be referred to a supervisor before determination. |
| Household member subject to immigration public recourse ban | **§5.5.2** | Case must be referred to a supervisor before determination. |
| Application undecided after 90 days from filing | **§8.3.3** | Application must be referred to a supervisor. |
| Allegation of deliberate misrepresentation / fraud | **§9.6.2** | Determination must be made by a supervisor based on recorded file evidence. |
| Suspension unresolved after 60 days | **§10.2.3** | Suspension must be referred to a supervisor for termination determination. |
